This is the research-control layer for the next VinMerge phase:

1. Positive vehicle identification.
2. Exact configuration identification.
3. OEM parts matching.
4. OE supersession.

## Evidence hierarchy

**Tier A — primary:** manufacturer VIN specifications/submissions; OEM EPC; OEM parts diagrams; OEM workshop/service information; OEM TSBs; manufacturer model/engine/transmission/production-break documentation.

**Tier B — industry/manufacturer:** TecDoc/TecRMI; manufacturer aftermarket catalogues; known OE cross-reference and supersession data.

**Tier C — structured secondary:** PartSouq, Amayama, CATCAR, 7zap and specialist catalogue interfaces. These are research evidence and must retain provenance.

**Tier D — discovery/corroboration:** GitHub datasets, Wikidata/Wikipedia, specialist forums, enthusiast communities, dealer/used-parts listings and visual sources. These cannot independently promote a fitment claim to verified.

## Verified resource observations

- ISO 3779:2009 remains the current international VIN content/structure standard.
- SAE J1044_202501 is the current revised WMI practice; SAE J272 has a 2024 revision.
- NHTSA vPIC provides manufacturer-reported VIN data and plant information, but it cannot be treated as universal East-African/JDM coverage.
- TecDoc provides vehicle/article/linkage data and VIN, OE-number and part-number search.
- Mercedes-Benz XENTRY Parts Information supports FIN/VIN-based replacement-part lookup and vehicle-specific lists by build year, model and equipment.
- PartSouq provides genuine catalogue interfaces across many major makes and VIN/frame/part-number search.
- Amayama exposes model-code, engine, grade, transmission, options and production-period context in OEM catalogue pages.
- CATCAR provides broad structured catalogue coverage across Japan, Korea, Europe and USA.

## Research strategy by family

### Japanese/JDM

Toyota, Lexus, Nissan, Infiniti, Suzuki, Mazda, Honda, Mitsubishi, Subaru, Isuzu and Daihatsu require VIN **and** frame/chassis/model-code research. JDM/import vehicles frequently need frame/model-code and market evidence in addition to generic VIN decoding.

### Korean

Hyundai, Kia and Genesis require OEM/Mobis-style parts evidence plus model-code, engine, transmission and market context.

### European

BMW/MINI, Mercedes/Smart, Volkswagen/Audi and Land Rover/Jaguar require build-date, equipment, engine, transmission and production-break awareness. French and Italian makes require equivalent platform/engine/production-period controls.

### North American

Ford/Lincoln, GM brands, Chrysler/Dodge/Jeep/Ram and Tesla require market-specific VIN and OEM parts evidence. NHTSA is useful but market-limited.

### Indian/regional

Tata, Mahindra, Ashok Leyland, Force Motors, Maruti Suzuki and Proton require regional OEM/dealer documentation, chassis/model codes and engine data.

### Chinese

FAW, Dongfeng, JAC, Foton, JMC, BYD, Changan, Chery, Geely, GWM/Haval, MG, BAIC, DFSK, GAC, Jetour, Omoda, Jaecoo, Exeed, Hongqi, Leapmotor and Neta require manufacturer/regional sources and strict market-year controls.

### Commercial vehicles

Hino, Fuso/Mitsubishi Fuso, Sinotruk/Howo, Scania, Volvo Trucks, MAN, FAW, Dongfeng, JAC, Foton, JMC, Iveco, DAF, Renault Trucks, UD Trucks and Maxus need a dedicated commercial-vehicle path: chassis, engine, axle, transmission and cab configuration must be treated as fitment-critical.

## Parts matching chain

**VIN/chassis/model code → exact configuration → OEM catalogue applicability → OEM part number → supersession → aftermarket manufacturer number → secondary corroboration → Fitment Master verification.**

Each relationship should retain canonical configuration, identifiers, market, production period/breakpoint, engine, transmission, drivetrain/body/grade where relevant, component, OEM number, supersession, aftermarket brand/number, source, source tier, date, evidence status, reviewer status and contradiction status.

## Non-negotiable rules

1. Model name alone never establishes fitment.
2. Chassis code alone does not establish VIN VDS semantics.
3. Shared engine does not imply shared part fitment.
4. Visual similarity does not establish interchangeability.
5. AI inference cannot establish authoritative VIN identity, exact configuration, OEM fitment or interchangeability.
6. Conflicting evidence remains unresolved until explicitly reviewed.
7. No uncertain candidate is promoted into verified Fitment Master knowledge.

## Work queues

**Q1 — Positive identity:** WMI/VDS/VIS/chassis/frame/model-code and manufacturer VIN semantics.

**Q2 — Exact configuration:** model-year, production breaks, engine/fuel, transmission, drivetrain, body, grade/trim, market and options.

**Q3 — OEM fitment:** configuration → OEM part using EPC/technical evidence.

**Q4 — Supersession:** old OE → replacement OE with effective periods where available.

**Q5 — Aftermarket:** OEM reference → manufacturer part → TecDoc/industry corroboration.

**Q6 — Contradictions:** create explicit research cases rather than guessing.

**Q7 — Measurement:** build representative VIN/chassis/configuration/part test sets and measure identity, configuration, OEM-fitment and aftermarket-fitment accuracy separately.

## Output policy

Research is additive. The authoritative 825-model master is not changed by this map. Evidence must pass provenance, contradiction and fitment review before promotion to verified knowledge.
